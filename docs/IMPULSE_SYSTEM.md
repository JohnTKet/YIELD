# YIELD — IMPULSE System

**IMPULSE** is YIELD's original superhuman energy system.

It is designed to provide a common foundation for extraordinary abilities while still allowing individual players to develop dramatically different characters.

Rather than selecting a predefined superhero or rigid character class, players develop their own relationship with IMPULSE through Affinity, techniques, Output, mastery, conditions, movement, and combat style.

> **IMPULSE defines the rules of power. The player defines how those rules are used.**

---

## Design Goals

IMPULSE is being designed around five primary goals:

1. **Personalization** — players sharing an Affinity should still be capable of developing very different builds.
2. **Skill Expression** — movement, timing, positioning, technique, and combat decisions should remain important.
3. **Meaningful Tradeoffs** — stronger effects can require greater energy, risk, commitment, or self-imposed restrictions.
4. **Mechanical Integration** — powers should interact with movement, combat, progression, and the environment rather than existing as isolated abilities.
5. **Long-Term Growth** — progression should expand what a player can accomplish without making lower-level opponents automatically irrelevant.

# The Six Affinities

IMPULSE manifests through six broad Affinities.

```text
                     IMPULSE
                        │
        ┌───────────────┼───────────────┐
        │               │               │
      DRIVE           VECTOR        PROJECTION
        │               │               │
        └───────────────┼───────────────┘
                        │
        ┌───────────────┼───────────────┐
        │               │               │
      FORGE         ALTERATION       ANOMALY
```

An Affinity represents a character's natural relationship with IMPULSE.

It does **not** define a complete moveset.

---

## DRIVE

**Domain:** Physical enhancement

DRIVE focuses IMPULSE through the user's body.

Potential applications include:

- Strength
- Durability
- Regeneration
- Speed
- Jumping
- Physical reinforcement
- Enhanced strikes
- Extreme physical output

A DRIVE user might specialize in overwhelming strength, while another could focus on speed, durability, recovery, or precise physical enhancement.

```text
DRIVE
├── Strength
├── Speed
├── Durability
├── Recovery
└── Physical Techniques
```

DRIVE is therefore not simply YIELD's "strength class."

It is the Affinity most naturally connected to **superhuman physical performance**.

---

## VECTOR

**Domain:** Movement and force

VECTOR specializes in controlling movement, acceleration, momentum, and force-related behavior.

Potential applications include:

- Extreme acceleration
- High-speed traversal
- Flight
- Directional boosting
- Air braking
- Momentum manipulation
- Force-based attacks
- Gravity-like effects
- Movement-focused techniques

A VECTOR user could become an extreme-speed fighter while another develops aerial mobility or force manipulation.

```text
VECTOR
├── Acceleration
├── Momentum
├── Flight
├── Force
└── Movement Techniques
```

VECTOR is designed to interact heavily with YIELD's movement systems.

---

## PROJECTION

**Domain:** Ranged IMPULSE release

PROJECTION specializes in releasing IMPULSE beyond the user's body.

Potential applications include:

- Energy blasts
- Beams
- Shockwaves
- Bursts
- Ranged pressure
- Focused projectiles
- Area attacks

Different users may emphasize precision, range, volume, power, efficiency, or unusual firing patterns.

```text
PROJECTION
├── Blasts
├── Beams
├── Shockwaves
├── Projectiles
└── Ranged Techniques
```

PROJECTION should not automatically mean standing at range and repeatedly firing the same attack.

Its techniques can still interact with movement, positioning, timing, and conditions.

---

## FORGE

**Domain:** IMPULSE construction

FORGE allows IMPULSE to take structured or constructed forms.

Potential applications include:

- Armor
- Weapons
- Barriers
- Shields
- Constructs
- Tools
- Drones
- Specialized equipment

```text
FORGE
├── Armor
├── Weapons
├── Barriers
├── Constructs
└── Devices
```

FORGE is intended to support highly specialized builds.

One player might develop personal armor while another creates weapons, defensive barriers, or autonomous constructs.

---

## ALTERATION

**Domain:** Changing properties

ALTERATION focuses on changing properties of the user's IMPULSE, body, or techniques.

Potential applications may include:

- Element-like properties
- Physical property changes
- Energy behavior changes
- Specialized body modifications
- Technique transformation
- Conditional state changes

```text
ALTERATION
├── Energy Properties
├── Body Properties
├── Technique Modification
└── Specialized States
```

ALTERATION is intended to provide deeper mechanical experimentation once players understand YIELD's core systems.

---

## ANOMALY

**Domain:** Unconventional IMPULSE behavior

ANOMALY exists for abilities that do not naturally belong within the other Affinities.

It is not intended to mean:

> "Anything goes."

ANOMALY techniques should still operate within YIELD's balance, progression, resource, condition, and combat systems.

Instead, ANOMALY provides design space for unusual rule interactions and highly specialized abilities.

```text
ANOMALY
└── Unconventional IMPULSE Behavior
```

Its purpose is to preserve creativity without forcing every possible technique into an inappropriate category.

# Foundation and Advanced Affinities

The current progression design divides Affinities into two groups.

### Foundation Affinities

```text
DRIVE
VECTOR
PROJECTION
```

These are intended to be available during the player's initial progression.

They are **not weaker Affinities**.

A highly developed DRIVE, VECTOR, or PROJECTION character should remain viable throughout the game.

### Advanced Affinities

```text
FORGE
ALTERATION
ANOMALY
```

These are planned as later progression unlocks because their mechanics can require a stronger understanding of IMPULSE.

Their later availability represents **complexity and expanded choice**, not automatic superiority.

---

# IMPULSE Breakthrough

The current design includes a future progression milestone called an **IMPULSE Breakthrough**.

After reaching an appropriate progression threshold, a player would become eligible for a Breakthrough challenge or trial.

Successful completion would unlock access to:

```text
FORGE
ALTERATION
ANOMALY
```

The exact level requirement and final trial design are **not yet locked**.

The current design direction is for this unlock to become account-wide so players can explore advanced Affinities across additional characters without repeating unnecessary progression barriers.

---

# Character Identity

A YIELD character is intended to be defined by more than Affinity.

The current design model is:

```text
Affinity
   +
Expression
   +
Technique
   +
Conditions
   +
Mastery
   +
Output
   ↓
Individual Combat Identity
```

### Affinity

What IMPULSE naturally does for the character.

### Expression

How that character naturally prefers to use their Affinity.

### Technique

A trained application of IMPULSE.

### Conditions

Voluntary rules or restrictions placed on techniques.

### Mastery

How efficiently and precisely the character can use their abilities.

### Output

How much power the character is actively releasing.

This structure is intended to prevent Affinity selection from becoming equivalent to selecting a predefined hero.

---

# Techniques

A **Technique** is a specific trained application of IMPULSE.

For example, two VECTOR players might share the same Affinity but develop entirely different technique libraries.

```text
VECTOR USER A
├── Extreme acceleration
├── Ground mobility
├── Momentum attacks
└── High-speed evasive techniques

VECTOR USER B
├── Flight
├── Air braking
├── Force redirection
└── Aerial combat
```

Both characters remain VECTOR users.

Their actual gameplay identities are different.

This principle applies across every Affinity.

---

# Limits and Conditions

Players may eventually place voluntary **Limits or Conditions** on techniques.

The core principle is:

> **Meaningful restriction can justify meaningful strength.**

A condition might restrict:

- Activation circumstances
- Duration
- Range
- Target
- Energy consumption
- Movement
- Timing
- Required setup
- Recovery
- Risk

For example, a technique that can only be used after achieving sufficient momentum may justify a stronger effect than an equivalent technique available instantly from a standstill.

The stronger the benefit, the more meaningful the restriction should be.

Conditions are intended to encourage:

- Theorycrafting
- Specialized builds
- Player identity
- Risk/reward decisions
- Creative combat strategies

They should not function as arbitrary free multipliers.

---

# IMPULSE Output

**IMPULSE Output** represents how much of a character's available power is actively being released.

Output is planned to influence multiple systems rather than functioning as a simple damage slider.

Potential interactions include:

```text
                    OUTPUT
                       │
      ┌────────────────┼────────────────┐
      ▼                ▼                ▼
   Strength          Speed           Abilities
      │                │                │
      └────────────────┼────────────────┘
                       ▼
               Energy Consumption
                       │
               VFX / Presence / Risk
```

Higher Output may provide greater performance while also increasing costs or risks depending on the character's build.

Potential consequences include:

- Increased IMPULSE consumption
- Reduced efficiency
- Greater visual intensity
- More difficult control
- Increased recovery requirements
- Technique-specific risk

Output is still under active design and has **not yet been fully implemented**.

---

# Momentum + IMPULSE

Movement is intended to matter mechanically.

YIELD's conceptual impact model is:

> **Base Power + Velocity/Momentum + IMPULSE Output + Technique + Ability Modifiers = Final Impact**

This is a design model rather than a literal final damage equation.

Its purpose is to establish that combat impact should depend on more than character level.

A player accelerating into an attack should not necessarily produce the same result as performing the same technique from a standstill.

Likewise, higher Output should not automatically compensate for poor technique, positioning, or timing.

# Progression Philosophy

YIELD is not intended to use progression where:

```text
Higher Level = Automatic Victory
```

Progression should increase capability while preserving the importance of:

- Player skill
- Movement
- Technique
- Build quality
- Timing
- Resource management
- Matchups
- Conditions
- Positioning

Potential progression attributes include:

```text
Strength
Speed
Durability
Stamina
Impulse Capacity
Output
Control
Recovery
Technique Mastery
Efficiency
Precision
```

The final progression formulas and balance are still under development.

---

# Character vs. Account Progression

YIELD is planned to distinguish between:

### Character Progression

The growth and specialization of an individual character.

### Account Progression

Broader unlocks representing player-level progression across YIELD.

This separation allows individual characters to maintain distinct builds while avoiding unnecessary repetition for major system unlocks.

Multiple character slots are planned so players can explore different Affinities and builds.

Additional character slots may eventually serve as a convenience option, but access to YIELD's core combat power should **not** depend on purchasing superior abilities.

---

# Combat Integration

IMPULSE is intended to integrate directly with YIELD's existing combat and movement architecture.

```text
Player Movement
      │
      ▼
Attack Context
      │
      ▼
Technique Selection
      │
      ▼
IMPULSE Output
      │
      ▼
Server Validation
      │
      ▼
Authoritative Contact
      │
      ▼
Final Combat Outcome
```

This allows abilities to build on the same server-authoritative foundation already established for standard combat.

The long-term objective is not to create one combat system for punches and a completely separate system for powers.

They should belong to the same combat language.

---

# Environmental Interaction

IMPULSE is also intended to affect the world.

Powerful attacks and abilities may eventually interact with staged environmental destruction:

```text
Normal
   ↓
Damaged
   ↓
Heavily Damaged
   ↓
Destroyed
   ↓
Rebuilt
```

This allows increasing power to become visually meaningful without requiring unrestricted physics destruction throughout the entire world.

Environmental destruction remains a planned system and is **not part of the current Foundation implementation**.

---

# Player Fantasy

The ultimate goal of IMPULSE is not simply to give players increasingly large attacks.

It is to allow players to gradually answer:

> **What kind of superhuman am I becoming?**

A player's Affinity provides the foundation.

Their techniques, movement, Output, conditions, mastery, and decisions provide the identity.

The intended progression fantasy is therefore:

```text
Discover
   ↓
Experiment
   ↓
Specialize
   ↓
Master
   ↓
Personalize
   ↓
Become Your Own Superhuman
```

# Current Implementation Status

IMPULSE already exists within YIELD's prototype as a gameplay resource supporting the movement foundation.

Current prototype work includes systems such as:

- IMPULSE resource management
- Consumption
- Regeneration
- Sprint interaction
- Impulse Burst
- High-speed movement

The broader system described in this document—including full Output mechanics, personalized techniques, Conditions, advanced Affinities, and Breakthrough progression—is **planned and under active design**.

This document describes the intended architecture without representing unfinished systems as completed features.
